# Refutation of `proposal-product-first.md`, through the EDGE CASE lens

Every numbered case in `01` part 1 (1 to 51), every refutation (R1 to R26) and every invariant
(I1 to I15), against what the proposal's own tables, guards and plugin rules would actually produce
for the rows the record measured. Citations are the input files: `00` engine facts, `01 case N` /
`01 RN` / `01 IN`, `02 section N`, `03 <source>`, `04 section N`. Proposal sections are cited as
`P section N`.

Verdicts: **HOLDS** (the stated rule produces the right edge for the real rows), **FIXABLE** (wrong or
missing, repairable inside the design), **FATAL** (the design cannot get it right without changing a
stated principle).

## Summary

### FATAL

1. **A dateless, retranslating, folding source can become a cluster MEMBER on count agreement alone, and member episodes place play buttons with no per-episode evidence.** Two roads reach it: `plugin:direct` accepts an `ask` `SAME_AS` from unogs whenever guard 7 finds the counts equal (guard 7 fires only on a MISMATCH, and no guard demands positive evidence), and `P 5.4 P4` rule 3 explicitly mints `SAME_AS` on "count equals `runLength` exactly ... or no date exists on their side". `01 R7` measured that axis as unable to separate two eleven-episode Demon Slayer runs sharing `nf:81091393-3`, and `01 case 11` measured the residue at 11 welds per 105 runs. Once the season is a member, `P 5.4 P5` numbers its episodes by `Episode.episodeNumber`, which for unogs is the positional index `i + 1` (`03 unogs`), so `01 case 15`'s insert and `01 case 16`'s duplicate rows put playable urls on the wrong rows: `01 R1`'s outcome by a different road, and a direct violation of the proposal's own principle 6 ("never on arithmetic") and G1. Fixing it needs a principle, not a patch: **a source whose profile says `retranslates` or which publishes no date at any level may never be a member; it may only be a container, and its episodes may only reach a slot through a proven `EPISODE_LINK`.**
2. **Migration step 5 deletes `lendContainingSeason` with nothing to replace the FETCH.** `pickSimilarSeason`'s fold veto refuses a season longer than the run with zero tolerance and `isRunAnswerFrom` refuses a non-RUN answer, so after step 5 no Crunchyroll Media or Episode row for the containing season ever enters the graph; `plugin:containment`'s span scan and `plugin:range` both require those rows to already be present. `01 case 12`'s measured win (both Mushoku part-twos going from 0 to 12 Crunchyroll sources) is lost. The design models the loan correctly and then removes the only thing that fetches it.
3. **A refusal that is not downgraded destroys data and creates a card.** Guards 4 and 5 write `LINK {status:'refused'}` and no usable edge. Since `CLAIMS` survives a refusal, `askUnasked` still fetches the target, `P 5.4 P5` puts every owned row in exactly one cluster, and `P 6.1` seeds every cluster: a refused `mal:51535` (AoT Final Chapters, `01 case 43`) becomes its own listing card while BOTH parts lose MAL's titles, its 0.9 declared count (the only top-tier witness, `01 I11`) and its cover. `01 I2`'s counterweight says refusing is not free and every refusal in the record carries a measured arm; these have none.

### SERIOUS (wrong or missing, fixable inside the design)

- **Case 10, the split direction**: "every numbered episode of theirs paired and their count is shorter" upgrades a catalogue season to `SAME_AS`, which is exactly the Fullmetal Alchemist / tvmaze-per-season shape the record says only exactness may admit. Needs the sibling test (no other season of the same `idParent` pairs onto a disjoint later range).
- **Case 8, `address` claims made inert**: `buildHandlesFromUri` is the only attach path for crunchyroll, unogs, appletv and justwatch rows resolved through an aggregated uri; making it inert turns each into a singleton cluster, and a singleton cluster is a card. Unpriced, where R8 priced the equivalent at 66 correct handles per 13 suspect.
- **Case 49, orphan cards**: every folding-origin season row that is only PART_OF a run is scope RUN and therefore never hidden; the record names this as an open blocker and the proposal adopts it as intended behaviour. Separately, making `hideAttachedContainers` store-wide can make a live-action show unfindable when its only run cluster is filtered out.
- **Case 45 / I9, `runLengthWitnesses` undefined**: tier-only or cluster-wide changes the answer on the two fixtures the record leans on, and the number gates both P4 rule 3 and the trim.
- **Case 30, `titleKeys STRING[]` drops the per-title score**, so the measured 69.6 / 99.9 / 70.0 selection arm cannot be implemented; the substitute (row score) is unmeasured.
- **Case 11 / I15, guard 5 is pass-scoped** while the scans are delta-scoped, so contested degenerates to first-in-wins.
- **The plugin contract contradicts itself**: `run()` returns the WHOLE desired output and the writer applies `DELETE current - desired`, yet `ctx.delta` exists so "a scan can be scoped". A scoped scan therefore retracts every edge outside the scope.
- **Membership by `reason:'aligned'` is non-monotonic** for a still-airing season, producing weekly cluster splits, `Cluster.id` churn (the urql cache key), `aggUri` shrinkage (against `04 contract 2`) and, through R15's key, fresh re-asks.
- **Case 16, an inflated list length** is read by guard 7, guard 8, P4 rule 3 and `INCLUDES.total`; `countKind` does not see it.
- **Case 44, anizip's missing row score is declared moot and is not**: ten scalar fields plus the season pair are still resolved by row score in `P 6.3`.
- **Case 43's stated failure mode is wrong about its own guards** (it refuses rather than welds), which means the proposal has not traced its most-cited per-run-origin case through its own writer.

### MINOR

- Case 3: `dateSubject` missing, so a show date on a season row is still indistinguishable; build year buckets from RUN members only.
- Case 6: minted links record no silent-veto state, so the case's own "must express" is only half met.
- Case 18: a container cluster can still build slots from a flat multi-season list; the protection lives in the extractors, not the representation.
- Case 22: per-origin tables (`countKind`, `folding`, `retranslates`, `showLevelOrigin`) have no entry and no declaration channel for a remote plugin source (`04 section 4.2`), and no default is stated.
- Case 39: `tmdb:550` collapses a film and a series onto one primary key; unaddressed, and the guard that could catch it (kind disagreement) does not exist.
- Case 20: the field-wise merge rule is unchanged, so a contaminated cluster still produces a right-looking row with one wrong field, now traceable.
- I7: `retranslates` is measured for Netflix only and asserted for nobody else; JustWatch republishing provider titles is untested.
- `LINK*0..8` in the cross-check and trace queries versus an uncapped JS union-find in P5: the two definitions of the closure disagree for chains over eight hops.
- `Answer.raw` is append-only, in memory, unbounded, and holds every resolver return including 200-episode lists from 24 sources; the replay and trace story rests on it and nothing bounds it.
- `P 6.1` returns every member's whole `raw` JSON for every cluster on the whole-store fallback read (`04 contract 6`), across the nested-worker boundary; 00's 49 ms figure is for 60 clusters of projections, not for this.

### HOLDS, the hardest cases it clearly handles

- **Case 6** (the season 1 to season 3 bridge): structurally unreachable, because the closure admits only `SAME_AS` and every bare catalogue id is a CONTAINER that can never be a member. This is the single most expensive defect in the record and the design kills the mechanism rather than the instance.
- **Case 1 and case 41** (one Crunchyroll series url on three Kitsu seasons, one film-collection id on fifteen films): `PART_OF` with the claimer's url carried on `CLAIMS.node`, so the badge survives with no identity claim and the edge is deletable.
- **Case 9, 12, 13, 14, 15 together** (the fold, the loan, the 13..20 renumbering, three numbering spaces, the mid-season insert): one `EPISODE_LINK` per episode carrying the shared day, `INCLUDES` with its range and `aligned/total`, and `FILLS.number` as the run's view over an untouched stored row. This is the requested primitive and it is expressed correctly; one insert costs one row its button.
- **Case 7 and I1** (one chokepoint, nothing irreversible): one writer, typed isolation, a per-pass source audit, and every merge a retractable edge with `by`, `reason`, `supports`.
- **Case 24, 25, 29, 33, 34, 26, R6, R17** (the whole title and date axis): one key computed once, ordinals as first-class attributes, silence never blocking, first-of-month dropped as a day but kept as a year, thresholds untouched.
- **Case 46, 47, 48, I5** (contradictions as a runtime signal, the seed not ratifying itself, claims before rows, provenance): all four are improvements over the current store rather than reproductions of it.

### Score

**6 / 10.** The edge model is the right shape and it kills the two most expensive mechanisms in the
record outright (the bare-container bridge and the irreversible union). It loses points because three
of the record's own measured cases come back wrong under its own rules: a dateless retranslating
source can become a member on a count coincidence and then place play buttons positionally (R1's
outcome, R7's numbers), migration step 5 deletes the only fetch that puts a containing season in the
graph (case 12 regresses to zero), and an undowngraded refusal both destroys the source's data and
mints a card (case 43, case 49, against I2). Each is repairable, but the first needs a principle
written down that the proposal currently contradicts.
